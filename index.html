What is Roast as a Service (RaaS)?

Welcome to Roastify, the internet's premier AI Roast Generator. Whether you need a hilarious reality check on your GitHub profile commit graph, an unhinged breakdown of your resume buzzwords, a critique of your startup pitch deck, or a teardown of your spaghetti code snippet, our system serves them piping hot.

RaaS is built on top of high-fidelity large language models (LLMs) like Google Gemini, wrapped in customized personas including Gordon Ramsay, a Silicon Valley VC Bro, a Condescending Code Reviewer, and a sarcastic Gen Z slacker. Customize your severity from a playful Mild poke to Spicy sarcasm, all the way to a Nuclear meltdown.

Core Features:

  • GitHub Profile Roaster: Extracts repos, languages, and followers to roast your developer credentials.
  • Resume Analyzer & Roaster: Dissects job histories and buzzwords to highlight corporate cringe.
  • Startup Teardown: Evaluates your pitch to target weak moats and CAC projections.
  • Spaghetti Code Blaster: Reviews complexity, nested loops, and structural debt.
  • Roast Battle Mode: Put two profiles head-to-head and let AI decide the victor!
